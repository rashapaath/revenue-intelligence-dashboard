# NovaTech Revenue Intelligence Dashboard
## Executive Summary

### Marketing Funnel

The sheet analyzes lead generation performance across campaign channels, campaign response rates, campaign ROI, and funnel stage distribution to provide a comprehensive view of marketing effectiveness.

- Partner Referral dominates lead generation with 757 distinct leads, nearly 20% more than Organic Search at 635.
- Direct Mail achieves the highest average campaign response at 0.62, more than double Partner Referral's 0.28, suggesting volume and engagement don't always align.
- All campaigns yield negative ROI, with the best performers—Digital Retarget and NovaPulse Launch—still at -0.9.
- Qualified Lead is the largest funnel stage at 788, indicating strong lead qualification despite underwhelming campaign returns.

### Sales Pipeline

The sheet provides an overview of sales performance, analyzing deal values by product and customer segment alongside sales rep efficiency in closing deals.

- NovaPulse Professional dominates deal value at 33,428,324, accounting for roughly a third of the total 100,900,682.
- Sales rep closing times vary dramatically, with Mei Lin Zhang averaging 126.23 days compared to Hannah Lee's swift 24 days.
- The Standard customer segment surprisingly leads in average deal value at 2,023.81, outperforming both Professional (1,572.39) and Enterprise (1,484.41) segments.
- NovaEdge Lite trails significantly with only 380,937 in total deal value.

### Customer Health View

The sheet provides an overview of customer support ticket performance, analyzing resolution times by priority, customer sentiment distribution, and ticket volume across product areas.

- Low priority issues surprisingly have the longest average resolution time at 2.05 days, exceeding both high (1.92 days) and medium (1.91 days) priorities.
- Customer sentiment is overwhelmingly neutral at 1,821 tickets, with negative sentiment (630) more than doubling positive sentiment (288), signaling a potential customer satisfaction concern.
- Notifications (567) and Analytics Dashboard (561) generate the most support tickets, while Data Pipeline (289) and Billing (305) report the fewest issues.
